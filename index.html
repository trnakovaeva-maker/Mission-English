Why STANAG Level 3?

NATO Standard

STANAG Level 3 represents professional working proficiency required for effective participation in multinational operations and NATO activities.

Career Advancement

Achieving B2 proficiency opens opportunities for international assignments, specialized training, and leadership positions in multinational contexts.

Professional Communication

Level 3 ensures you can communicate clearly in professional situations, understand complex texts, and express ideas effectively in English.

Practical Skills
